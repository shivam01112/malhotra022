About Us

ABOUT MALHOTRA ENGINEERING

WHAT WE DO

Malhotra Engineering caters to the complete range requirements — from hatchbacks to luxury modern vehicles. We manufacture hydraulic brake parts, rubber-to-metal bonded components, and precision rubber parts, delivering durable and high-performance solutions across domestic and international markets.

The company offers a comprehensive range of components for heavy-duty commercial vehicles, trailers, earthmoving equipment, material handling systems, and agricultural vehicles, supporting Indian, European, Japanese, Chinese, and American applications.
We specialize in custom new builds and precise reverse engineering.
